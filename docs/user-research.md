# User Research

This prototype is based on observed agency workflow pain points:

- Briefs often arrive incomplete or scattered across messages.
- Proposal writing repeats the same sections across clients.
- Formatting a PDF can take longer than writing the core offer.
- Clear scope, timeline, and pricing presentation increase trust.

Research still needed:

- Interviews with agency founders and sales teams.
- Testing with businesses submitting real project briefs.
- Measurement of time saved compared with manual proposal writing.
